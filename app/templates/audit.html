Fairness Audit Methodology

1. Demographic Segmentation: Students grouped by gender, nationality, GPA band, and current scholarship status
2. Match Rate Calculation: For each group, we calculate average scholarships matched per student
3. Bias Detection: If max match rate - min match rate > {{ "%.2f"|format(threshold) }} matches/student, a bias alert is triggered
4. Diversity Score: Measures concentration of matches (1 = perfect diversity, 0 = all matches to one group)
Based on: Rasooli et al. (2023) - Fairness in Educational Assessment, Ziegler et al. (2021) - Algorithmic Fairness in Education
